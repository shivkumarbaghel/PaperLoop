PaperLoop — The Problem

India's regional newspapers are invisible — and inaccessible — digitally.

2,400+ daily newspapers. 110M+ Hindi/regional readers. Yet the digital reading experience is broken for everyone.

Print-to-digital friction

Publishers invest lakhs in printing yet earn almost nothing online. PDF e-papers don't monetize — they just exist.

👤

Non-English readers abandoned

Hindi and regional readers — 600M+ citizens — get English-first apps. No reader community. No personalization built for them.

📱

Articles buried in raster scans

Even when publishers go digital, articles are trapped inside scanned page images. No links. No engagement. No analytics.

PaperLoop — Our Solution

A complete e-akhabaar platform for India's regional publishers and readers.

From scanned page to publishable article post — with community, subscription, and analytics built in.

1

AI Extracts Articles from E-Paper Pages

Publisher uploads PDF → AI detects articles, headlines, authors → generates structured article post + clipped image.

2

Editor Reviews & Publishes

Dashboard shows AI-suggested blocks on each page. Editor approves, edits, or rejects. Publishes to city feed.

3

Readers Discover & Engage

Browse by city + language. E-paper viewer with tap-to-article hotspots. Comment, like, share, follow publishers.

4

Revenue: Subscriptions + Ads

Readers subscribe to specific publishers. Publishers see real analytics. Advertisers buy targeted placement.
