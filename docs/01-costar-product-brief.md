# PaperLoop COSTAR Product Brief

Last updated: 2026-05-26

## Context

Traditional print newspapers are losing reach and revenue as readers move to digital channels, social feeds, video, and aggregators. At the same time, many readers still value the ritual of a newspaper: editions, pages, local identity, columnists, and the feeling of scanning a complete daily issue.

Existing e-paper products show that the market already understands page replicas, date archives, clipping, subscriptions, sharing, and publisher upload workflows. The opportunity for PaperLoop is to combine the familiar newspaper edition with article-level digital interaction and a subscriber community.

Primary launch context:

- India/Hindi-first for local and regional newspapers.
- Publisher-first, because the supply side needs tools to upload, manage, monetize, and analyze editions.
- Web-first MVP for desktop and mobile browsers.
- Manual editor review remains part of the publishing workflow, even if OCR or AI support is used.

## Objective

PaperLoop should help newspaper agencies transform daily print-style editions into a digital experience where:

- Publishers upload full e-paper issues and manage editions, cities, pages, sections, ads, subscriptions, and analytics.
- Editors convert page areas into individual article posts.
- Readers browse the full newspaper page, open clipped stories, save/share articles, and discuss news with other subscribers.
- Columnists build profiles, publish or claim article posts, and respond to reader engagement.
- Moderators protect discussion quality through reporting, review, and publisher-controlled visibility.
- Super admins manage agencies, platform subscriptions, cross-publisher analytics, user access, advertising inventory, and platform-wide controls.
- Readers discover newspapers through a dashboard organized by leading publications, language, region, popularity, relevance, and topics.

The product should not become only a PDF viewer or only a social feed. Its core identity is the bridge between the complete newspaper edition and an interactive article/community layer.

## Style

The experience should feel like a trustworthy digital newspaper workspace, not a generic social app.

Product style principles:

- Familiar newspaper structure: editions, dates, pages, sections, cities, and columns.
- Newspaper discovery that feels like a digital newsstand, with prominent leading newspapers and clear filters for regional/local discovery.
- Fast article access: page hotspots and clipped article posts should make reading easier than zooming into a static PDF.
- Respectful community: comments and reactions should support informed discussion, not performative engagement.
- Publisher control: publishers decide what is public, subscriber-only, archived, monetized, or moderated.
- Hindi-first usability: labels, date formatting, city/edition selection, and article reading should support Hindi newspapers from the beginning.
- Multimedia should enhance reporting with video, galleries, interactive graphics, and digital ad units without hiding the newspaper edition.

## Tone

PaperLoop should sound clear, credible, local, and reader-friendly.

Recommended tone:

- For readers: accessible, calm, and community-oriented.
- For publishers: practical, revenue-aware, and operationally clear.
- For columnists: professional, identity-building, and conversation-friendly.
- For moderators/admins: precise, auditable, and low-drama.

Avoid:

- Sensational engagement language.
- Overly technical publisher onboarding copy.
- Social-media-first terminology that weakens the newspaper identity.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Newspaper publishers and agencies that need a web platform for digitizing and monetizing e-paper editions.

Secondary audiences:

- Editors who clip articles, correct OCR, assign sections, and manage article visibility.
- Readers and subscribers who want the daily newspaper online with better article access.
- Columnists who want direct connection with readers around their published work.
- Moderators who manage comments, reports, and discussion quality.
- Advertisers who want page-level, article-level, or edition-level placements.

## Response / Result

The planned MVP should produce a working product direction with these outcomes:

- A super admin can create and manage publisher/agency accounts, plans, users, platform analytics, and ad inventory.
- A publisher can upload an issue and publish it as a browsable e-paper edition.
- An editor can define article hotspots on pages and create article posts linked back to the original page.
- A reader can browse by date, city, edition, page, section, language, region, topic, popularity, and relevance.
- A subscriber can save, share, comment on, and follow discussions around articles.
- A columnist can have a verified profile linked to their article posts.
- A moderator can review reported comments and apply publisher-defined moderation actions.
- A publisher can track basic edition, article, subscriber, and engagement analytics.
- A marketing/admin team can analyze reach, engagement, feedback, and revenue to improve content strategy and subscriber campaigns.

## Success Criteria

PaperLoop is successful when it proves three linked behaviors:

- Publishers can digitize editions without heavy technical support.
- Readers prefer article-linked e-paper reading over static PDF/page-only reading.
- Subscriber discussions and columnist profiles increase retention without damaging trust or moderation quality.
